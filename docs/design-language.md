# Design language

## Goal

A creator/gaming broadcast aesthetic that feels deliberately designed, not like a generic AI/SaaS dashboard.

## Direction

- Warm off-white surfaces against near-black/navy rather than pure neon-on-black everywhere.
- One strong purple accent and one warm yellow accent.
- Thick rules, compact uppercase labels, oversized editorial score typography.
- Slightly irregular/physical visual details: offset hard shadows, sticker-like badges, decisive borders.
- Minimal gradients. Avoid glassmorphism, excessive glow, and giant rounded cards nested inside giant rounded cards.
- Match-result screens take cues from sports broadcast comparison graphics: two competitors anchored left/right, shared stat/game in the center, winner marks on the appropriate side.
- Game artwork supplies most of the color once real catalog assets are integrated.

## Ludwig inspiration without impersonation

Borrow the playful creator-event energy, punchy typography, warm merchandise-like palette, and competitive-show presentation. Do not use Ludwig/Mogul Moves logos, exact marks, exact trade dress, or imply affiliation. The pitch to Ludwig should be easy to re-skin collaboratively if he is interested.
